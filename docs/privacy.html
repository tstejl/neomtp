This page describes how OpenMTP handles data in the Service.

OpenMTP uses local data to provide and maintain the Service. The app does not collect telemetry, analytics, or usage data.

Information Collection And Use

OpenMTP does not collect personal data, anonymous usage data, or telemetry. The app does not automatically send your data or diagnostic logs to remote services.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data OpenMTP does not ask you to provide personally identifiable information that can contact or identify you ("Personal Data").

Cookies: Cookies are small data files that websites can store in a browser.

This Service does not use cookies. OpenMTP does not use third-party cookies, analytics, or tracking tools to collect information.

Usage Data: The application does not send usage analytics or crash reports to remote services.

LocalStorage Data: The application uses local storage to remember your preferences and settings. These files remain on your device.

LocalStorage files we use in the app:
  • Settings File. We use Settings Files to remember your preferences and various settings.
  • Log Files. The application stores diagnostic logs locally after errors occur. The application does not send these logs to remote services.

Use of Data

OpenMTP uses local data only to provide and maintain the Service:

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To notify you about changes to our Service
  • To allow you to use interactive features of our Service
  • To provide customer care and support
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues

Transfer Of Data

OpenMTP keeps local data on your device. The app does not transfer personal data, usage data, analytics, telemetry, or diagnostic logs to remote services.

The app transfers files only between your computer and the connected Android device that you select.

You can choose to send diagnostic logs by email with a problem report.

Disclosure Of Data

OpenMTP does not receive or keep your personal data, files, usage data, or diagnostic logs.

Security Of Data

OpenMTP stores local data on your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Service Providers

OpenMTP does not use third-party services to collect, analyze, or store your usage data.

The app does not send personal data, files, or diagnostic logs to third-party services.

Links To Other Sites

Our Service contains links to sites that we do not operate. Selecting a third-party link opens that site in your browser.

Review the Privacy Policy of each site that you visit.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

Internet Activity

The app sends requests to GitHub.com to check for updates and to test the internet connection.

These requests do not include your files, usage data, or diagnostic logs.

You can disable automatic update checks. Open "Settings", then turn off "Enable auto-update check".

Please refer to  https://help.github.com/articles/github-privacy-statement/  for more information.

Crash Reports

The application stores diagnostic logs locally after errors occur.

The application does not send these logs automatically.

You can access the logs in "$HOME/Library/Application Support/io.ganeshrvel.openmtp/logs/".

You can choose to send these logs with a problem report.

Open the "Help" menu, then select "Report Bugs". Select the "EMAIL ERROR LOGS" button.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 18 ("Children").

OpenMTP does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 18.
